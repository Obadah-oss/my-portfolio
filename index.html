PROJECT 01
GRADUATION PROJECT

Multi-Area Load Frequency Control

Modeled and analyzed Load Frequency Control (LFC) to investigate frequency deviations and power exchange in interconnected power systems following load disturbances.

Key Objectives

  • Studied single-area, two-area, and three-area power system configurations.
  • Compared conventional integral control with advanced strategies, including LQR, LQG, Optimal Control, and IMC-PID.
  • Developed a three-area LFC simulation in MATLAB/Simulink at a nominal frequency of 60 Hz.
  • Analyzed system responses to a 200 MW load disturbance in Area 2 and calculated theoretical frequency deviations and mechanical power changes.
